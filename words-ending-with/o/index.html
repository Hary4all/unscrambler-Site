Hook Plays: Using -O Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in O are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 3,573 words that end in O gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
