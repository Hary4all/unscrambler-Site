Hook Plays: Using -Y Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in Y are valuable for hook plays - placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 34,136 words that end in Y gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
