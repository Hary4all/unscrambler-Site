Hook Plays: Using -H Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in H are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 6,000 words that end in H gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
