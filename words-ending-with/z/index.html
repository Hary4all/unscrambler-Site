Hook Plays: Using -Z Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in Z are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 251 words that end in Z gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
