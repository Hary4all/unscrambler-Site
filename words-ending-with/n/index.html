Hook Plays: Using -N Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in N are valuable for hook plays - placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 24,550 words that end in N gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
