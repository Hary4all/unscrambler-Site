Hook Plays: Using -R Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in R are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 20,561 words that end in R gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
