Hook Plays: Using -U Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in U are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 811 words that end in U gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
