Frequently asked questions

What is the most common word ending in English?

-ED is the most common ending in our dictionary (22,223 words), followed by -ING (17,845) and -LY (15,012).

Which letter do most English words end with?

S ends the most words (72,683), mostly plurals, followed by E and Y.

Are these lists useful for rhymes?

Yes. Words with the same ending often rhyme, so the suffix pages are a quick rhyme finder.
