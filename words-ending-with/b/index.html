Hook Plays: Using -B Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in B are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 637 words that end in B gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
