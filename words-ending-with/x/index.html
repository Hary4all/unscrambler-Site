Hook Plays: Using -X Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in X are valuable for hook plays - placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 1,017 words that end in X gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
