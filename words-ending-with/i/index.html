Hook Plays: Using -I Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in I are valuable for hook plays - placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 3,366 words that end in I gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
