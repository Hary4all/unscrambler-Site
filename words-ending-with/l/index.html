Hook Plays: Using -L Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in L are valuable for hook plays — placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 17,475 words that end in L gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
