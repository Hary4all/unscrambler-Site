Hook Plays: Using -M Endings in Scrabble

Words ending in M are valuable for hook plays - placing your word so that its last letter connects with or extends an existing board word. This scores multiple words simultaneously. Knowing all 10,105 words that end in M gives you the maximum number of available hook positions on any board state.
