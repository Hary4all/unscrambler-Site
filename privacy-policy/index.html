Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

WordFindLab ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website at wordfindlab.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our site.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our tools. We may collect:

  • Usage data - pages visited, time on site, browser type, and device type, collected automatically via analytics
  • Cookies - small files stored by your browser used by advertising partners (see Section 4)
  • Local storage - your browser may cache our dictionary file locally for faster load times. This data never leaves your device.
  • Server-side cache - we may store dictionary lookups and word data on our backend to speed up repeat requests.

3. How We Use Information

Usage data is used solely to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the web. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. For more information about how Google uses your data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. Third-Party Services

Our site may load word data from public dictionary and word-list sources such as jsDelivr CDN and the Free Dictionary API. We may also use our own backend cache to reduce repeat lookups.

6. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You may opt out of cookies at any time through your browser settings. Since we do not collect personal accounts, there is no personal data to delete or export.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at: support@wordfindlab.com
