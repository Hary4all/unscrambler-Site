Daily Word Scramble

One new word every day. Unscramble the letters, keep your streak alive and share your result. It takes about two minutes.

Play more scrambles

How to play

  • Rearrange the letters on the tiles to make one everyday English word.
  • Type your answer and press Check. You can guess as many times as you like.
  • Stuck? Press Shuffle to see the letters in a new order, or use a hint: a clue first, then the first letter, then the answer.
  • Come back tomorrow for a new word and keep your streak going.

Tips for unscrambling words fast

  • Spot common pairs. Letter pairs such as TH, CH, SH, ER, ING and LE often belong together.
  • Put vowels between consonants. Most English words alternate vowels and consonants fairly evenly.
  • Try likely endings. Words often end in -ER, -LE, -ET, -EN or -Y.
  • Say it out loud. Sounding out possible chunks helps your brain recognise the word.

Frequently asked questions

When does a new word appear?

A new scramble unlocks every day at midnight in your own time zone. Everyone gets the same word on the same date.

How do the hints work?

You can use up to three hints: a clue, the first letter, and finally the answer itself. Solving without hints keeps your no-hint record going.

How is my streak counted?

Your streak grows by one for each day in a row you solve the puzzle. Miss a day and it starts again. Streaks are saved in your browser on this device.

Is the daily word scramble suitable for kids?

Yes. Every answer is an everyday word of five to eight letters, so it works well for families and classrooms.

I want more than one puzzle a day. Where can I play?

Try our themed word scrambles for Thanksgiving and Christmas, the word search for kids, or the daily crossword.
