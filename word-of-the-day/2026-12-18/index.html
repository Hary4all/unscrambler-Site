Meretricious

adjective

Definition

Apparently attractive but having in reality no value or integrity; tawdry. Something meretricious promises more than it can deliver.

Example Sentence

“The proposal was dazzlingly presented but ultimately meretricious — style covering a lack of substance.”

Etymology

From Latin meretricius, from meretrix (a prostitute), from mereri (to earn).
