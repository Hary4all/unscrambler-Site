Predilection

noun

Definition

A preference or special liking for something; a bias in favour of something. A predilection for something guides choices even when the person is unaware of it.

Example Sentence

“His predilection for underdog causes led him into a career in human rights law.”

Etymology

From French predilection, from Medieval Latin praedilectio, from praediligere (to prefer), from prae- (before) + diligere (to love).
