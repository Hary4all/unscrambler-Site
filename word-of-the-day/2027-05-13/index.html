Sempiternal

adjective

Definition

Everlasting; without end. The sempiternal quality of the best music makes each hearing feel like the first.

Example Sentence

“The sempiternal stars looked down on the night's events with their customary indifference.”

Etymology

From Late Latin sempiternalis, from sempiternus (everlasting), from semper (always) + aeternus (eternal).
