Sepulchral

adjective

Definition

Relating to a tomb; very dismal or dark; suggestive of death. A sepulchral voice is low, hollow, and gloomy.

Example Sentence

“The old man spoke in a sepulchral whisper that chilled everyone in the room.”

Etymology

From Latin sepulchralis, from sepulcrum (tomb), from sepelire (to bury).
