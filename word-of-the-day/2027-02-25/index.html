Eleemosynary

adjective

Definition

Relating to or dependent on charity. Eleemosynary institutions survive on the generosity of donors rather than earned income.

Example Sentence

“The hospital had been an eleemosynary institution for three centuries before the government took it over.”

Etymology

From Medieval Latin eleemosynarius, from eleemosyna (alms), from Greek eleemosune (pity), from eleemon (compassionate).
