Inimical

adjective

Definition

Tending to obstruct or harm; unfriendly; hostile. An inimical environment is one that works against success or survival.

Example Sentence

“The damp and cold of the cellar proved inimical to preserving the documents.”

Etymology

From Late Latin inimicalis, from Latin inimicus (hostile), from in- (not) + amicus (friend).
