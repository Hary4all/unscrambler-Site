Felicitous

adjective

Definition

Well chosen or suited to the circumstances; pleasing and fortunate. A felicitous phrase is one that says exactly the right thing in exactly the right way.

Example Sentence

“Her felicitous choice of words turned a difficult situation into a moment of unexpected grace.”

Etymology

From Latin felicitas (happiness), from felix (happy, fortunate).
