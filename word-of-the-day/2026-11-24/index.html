Sycophancy

noun

Definition

Obsequious flattery for personal gain; the behaviour of a sycophant. Sycophancy corrodes honest communication in any organisation.

Example Sentence

“The sycophancy of the courtiers blinded the monarch to the real state of his kingdom.”

Etymology

From Greek sukophantia, from sukophantes (informer), possibly from sukon (fig) + phainein (to show).
