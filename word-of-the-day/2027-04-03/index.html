Ephemeral

adjective

Definition

Lasting for a very short time. The most vivid experiences are often the most ephemeral.

Example Sentence

“The ephemeral perfection of the moment could not be held; he simply tried to remember every detail.”

Etymology

From Greek ephemeros, from epi- (on) + hemera (day).
