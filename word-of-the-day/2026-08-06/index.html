Obdurate

adjective

Definition

Stubbornly refusing to change one's opinion or course of action; hardened against persuasion. An obdurate person is immovable.

Example Sentence

“Despite overwhelming evidence, the obdurate defendant refused to change his plea.”

Etymology

From Latin obduratus, past participle of obdurare (to harden), from ob- (against) + durare (to harden).
