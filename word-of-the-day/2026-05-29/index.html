Seraphic

adjective

Definition

Characteristic of or resembling a seraph; angelic, blissful, or serene. A seraphic smile is one of pure, innocent happiness.

Example Sentence

“The infant wore a seraphic expression as he slept in his mother's arms.”

Etymology

From Late Latin seraphicus, from seraph, a six-winged celestial being described in the Bible.
