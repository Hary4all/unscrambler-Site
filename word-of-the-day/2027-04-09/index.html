Crepuscular

adjective

Definition

Of, relating to, or active at twilight. The crepuscular half-hour between day and night is the most richly coloured of all.

Example Sentence

“The crepuscular hour brought the foxes out of the woods and into the fields.”

Etymology

From Latin crepusculum (twilight), from creper (dusky).
