Vituperative

adjective

Definition

Bitter and abusive in speech or writing. A vituperative attack is one that goes beyond criticism to personal assault.

Example Sentence

“The vituperative editorial sparked a scandal that ended the journalist's career.”

Etymology

From Latin vituperativus, from vituperare (to blame, find fault with).
