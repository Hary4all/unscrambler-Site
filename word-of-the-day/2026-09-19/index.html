Redolent

adjective

Definition

Strongly reminiscent or suggestive of something; having a strong distinctive smell. Redolent surroundings carry scents or associations that immediately evoke the past.

Example Sentence

“The dusty attic was redolent of camphor and old wood, transporting her instantly to childhood.”

Etymology

From Old French redolent, from Latin redolens, from redolere (to emit a scent), from re- + olere (to smell).
