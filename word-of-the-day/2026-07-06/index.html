Bucolic

adjective

Definition

Relating to the pleasant aspects of the countryside and rural life; pastoral. A bucolic landscape is one of peaceful fields and meadows.

Example Sentence

“The writer retreated to a bucolic cottage to finish the final chapters of his novel.”

Etymology

From Latin bucolicus, from Greek boukolikos, from boukolos (cowherd), from bous (ox) + -kolos (herder).
