Celeritous

adjective

Definition

Characterized by celerity; moving or acting with speed. A celeritous response to a crisis can prevent escalation.

Example Sentence

“The celeritous intervention of the paramedics was credited with saving his life.”

Etymology

From Latin celeritas (speed), from celer (swift) + -ous.
