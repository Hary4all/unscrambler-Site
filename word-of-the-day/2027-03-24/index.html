Turpitude

noun

Definition

Wickedness; depravity. Moral turpitude is a legal concept but also a powerful moral one.

Example Sentence

“The investigation revealed a pattern of turpitude that extended from the boardroom to the factory floor.”

Etymology

From Latin turpitudo, from turpis (base, shameful).
