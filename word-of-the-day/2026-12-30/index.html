Halcyon

adjective

Definition

Denoting a period that was idyllically happy and peaceful. Halcyon days are those we look back on with a kind of sweet pain.

Example Sentence

“Those halcyon months before the move were treasured for years afterward.”

Etymology

From Greek alkuon (kingfisher), said to calm the seas during winter solstice.
