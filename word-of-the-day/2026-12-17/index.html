Lissome

adjective

Definition

Gracefully slender and flexible; lithesome. Lissome athletes make the most difficult movements look effortless.

Example Sentence

“The young gymnast was lissome and fearless, a combination that made her extraordinary to watch.”

Etymology

From an alteration of lithesome, from lithe + -some.
