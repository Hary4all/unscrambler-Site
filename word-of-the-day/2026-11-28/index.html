Nimiety

noun

Definition

An excess of something; redundancy. Nimiety occurs wherever more is provided than is necessary.

Example Sentence

“The report suffered from a nimiety of footnotes that obscured rather than supported its argument.”

Etymology

From Late Latin nimietas, from Latin nimius (too much), from nimis (too much).
