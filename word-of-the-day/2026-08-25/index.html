Candour

noun

Definition

The quality of being open and honest; frankness. Candour in leadership builds trust where flattery destroys it.

Example Sentence

“She admired his candour in admitting what the other candidates refused to acknowledge.”

Etymology

From Latin candor (whiteness, brilliance), from candere (to shine, be white).

Scrabble Score
