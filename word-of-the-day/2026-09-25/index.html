Veracious

adjective

Definition

Speaking or representing the truth; truthful and accurate. A veracious account is one that can be trusted as an honest representation of events.

Example Sentence

“The investigator was known for delivering veracious reports even when the truth was inconvenient.”

Etymology

From Latin verax (truthful), from verus (true).
