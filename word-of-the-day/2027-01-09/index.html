Catharsis

noun

Definition

The process of releasing and providing relief from strong or repressed emotions. A cathartic experience leaves the person lighter and cleaner.

Example Sentence

“The film offered a genuine catharsis — audiences wept freely and left feeling strangely refreshed.”

Etymology

From Greek katharsis (cleansing, purification), from kathairein (to cleanse), from katharos (pure).
