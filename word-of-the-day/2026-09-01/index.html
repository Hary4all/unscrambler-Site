Lachrymose

adjective

Definition

Tearful or given to weeping; inducing tears. A lachrymose novel is one likely to make readers cry.

Example Sentence

“The lachrymose farewell at the airport gate lasted almost an hour.”

Etymology

From Latin lachrymosus, from lachrima (a tear).
