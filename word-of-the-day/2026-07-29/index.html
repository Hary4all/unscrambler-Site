Sibilant

adjective

Definition

Making or characterized by a hissing sound; of or relating to the letters s and z. As a noun it refers to a sibilant consonant.

Example Sentence

“The sibilant whisper of the grass in the wind was the only sound on the moor.”

Etymology

From Latin sibilans, present participle of sibilare (to hiss).
