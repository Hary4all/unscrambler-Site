Perennial

adjective

Definition

Lasting or existing for a long or apparently infinite time; enduring. A perennial problem is one that keeps coming back year after year.

Example Sentence

“Traffic congestion is a perennial problem in the city centre during rush hour.”

Etymology

From Latin perennis (lasting through the year), from per- (through) + annus (year).
