Quixotic

adjective

Definition

Extremely idealistic and impractical, often in pursuit of unrealistic goals. The word conveys a kind of naive, romantic heroism divorced from reality.

Example Sentence

“His quixotic plan to walk across every continent by age thirty was met with admiring skepticism.”

Etymology

Named after Don Quixote, the chivalrous but deluded hero of Cervantes' 17th-century novel.
