Impecunious

adjective

Definition

Having little or no money; penniless. An impecunious student survives on ingenuity as much as income.

Example Sentence

“The impecunious young artist supplemented his income by teaching painting on weekends.”

Etymology

From Latin im- (not) + pecuniosus (wealthy), from pecunia (money), from pecus (cattle, which were once a measure of wealth).
