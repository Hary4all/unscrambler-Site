Clandestine

adjective

Definition

Kept secret or done secretively, especially because illicit. Clandestine operations are carried out in secret to avoid detection.

Example Sentence

“The spies used clandestine meetings in museum cafes for their dead-drop exchanges.”

Etymology

From Latin clandestinus, from clam (secretly), related to celare (to hide).
