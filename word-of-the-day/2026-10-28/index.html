Limpidity

noun

Definition

The quality of being transparent and clear; pellucid clarity. In writing or speech it means a freedom from obscurity or complexity.

Example Sentence

“The limpidity of the mountain lake was so complete that the reflection was nearly perfect.”

Etymology

From Latin limpidus (clear, transparent) + -ity.
