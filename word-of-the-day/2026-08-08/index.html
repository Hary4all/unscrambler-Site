Ennui

noun

Definition

A feeling of listlessness and dissatisfaction arising from a lack of occupation or excitement; boredom. It implies a deeper spiritual emptiness than mere boredom.

Example Sentence

“The endless afternoons of the school holiday eventually gave way to a deep, creeping ennui.”

Etymology

From French ennui, from Old French enui, from enuier (to be annoying), from Latin inodiare.
