Loquacious

adjective

Definition

Tending to talk a great deal; very talkative. A loquacious person rarely runs out of things to say and may talk more than the situation calls for.

Example Sentence

“Our loquacious guide filled every moment of the tour with fascinating commentary.”

Etymology

From Latin loquax, from loqui (to speak).
