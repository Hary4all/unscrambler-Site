Ineffable

adjective

Definition

Beyond expression in words; inexpressible. It is both honest and appropriate to say that some things are ineffable.

Example Sentence

“The ineffable sadness of the last movement moved every member of the audience in a different way.”

Etymology

From Latin ineffabilis, from in- (not) + effabilis (utterable).
