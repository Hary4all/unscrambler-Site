Munificent

adjective

Definition

More generous than is usual or expected; lavishly generous. A munificent patron gives beyond what is asked or expected.

Example Sentence

“The munificent donation endowed a new wing of the hospital.”

Etymology

From Latin munificus, from munus (gift) + facere (to make).
