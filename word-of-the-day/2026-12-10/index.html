Tenebrous

adjective

Definition

Dark and gloomy. Tenebrous writing creates an atmosphere of thick, unrelieved shadow.

Example Sentence

“The tenebrous gothic novel was set in a crumbling house where daylight never quite reached.”

Etymology

From Latin tenebrosus, from tenebrae (darkness).
