Palimpsest

noun

Definition

A manuscript page that has been written on, scraped clean, and reused; something that shows traces of earlier versions. It is a powerful metaphor for memory and history.

Example Sentence

“The city itself is a palimpsest, each new building bearing traces of what stood before.”

Etymology

From Greek palimpsestos, from palin (again) + psaein (to scrape smooth).
