Luminesce

verb

Definition

To emit light without heat, typically by chemical or biological processes; to glow. Fireflies and deep-sea creatures luminesce to communicate and hunt.

Example Sentence

“In the darkness, the plankton began to luminesce, turning every wave into a sheet of blue fire.”

Etymology

From Latin lumen (light) + -esce (to begin to do something).
