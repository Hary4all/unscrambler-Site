Opulent

adjective

Definition

Ostentatiously rich and luxurious or lavish. An opulent setting is one of extravagant wealth and display.

Example Sentence

“The opulent ballroom with its crystal chandeliers left guests breathless.”

Etymology

From Latin opulentus, from ops (wealth, resources).
