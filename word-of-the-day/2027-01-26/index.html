Turbid

adjective

Definition

Cloudy, opaque, or thick with suspended matter. Turbid water and turbid prose share the same quality of obscuring what lies beneath.

Example Sentence

“The turbid river ran brown and fast after three days of heavy rain in the hills.”

Etymology

From Latin turbidus (confused, disturbed), from turba (crowd, disturbance).
