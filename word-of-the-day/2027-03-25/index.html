Pellucid

adjective

Definition

Very clear; allowing light through. Pellucid writing allows the reader to see the ideas without seeing the prose.

Example Sentence

“His pellucid argument made the jury's decision feel almost preordained.”

Etymology

From Latin pellucidus, from per- (through) + lucere (to shine).
