Tautology

noun

Definition

The saying of the same thing twice in different words; needless repetition. A tautology adds no new information.

Example Sentence

“'Free gift' is a classic tautology — all gifts are, by definition, free.”

Etymology

From Late Latin tautologia, from Greek tautologos, from tauto- (same) + legein (to say).
