Melancholy

noun

Definition

A feeling of pensive sadness, typically with no obvious cause; thoughtful contemplation. As an adjective it describes a deeply pensive, sad mood.

Example Sentence

“A gentle melancholy settled over the town as autumn stripped the last leaves from the trees.”

Etymology

From Greek melankholia, from melas (black) + khole (bile); in ancient medicine, an excess of black bile caused sadness.
