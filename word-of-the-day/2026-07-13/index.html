Numinous

adjective

Definition

Having a strong religious or spiritual quality; indicating or suggesting the presence of a divinity; mysterious and awe-inspiring. A numinous experience is one that feels sacred.

Example Sentence

“The ancient stone circle had a numinous atmosphere that silenced even the most sceptical visitors.”

Etymology

From Latin numen (divine will, presence), from nuere (to nod, beckon).
