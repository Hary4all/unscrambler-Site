Fulminate

verb

Definition

To express vehement protest; to make a violent verbal attack. Fulminators are often more heat than light.

Example Sentence

“The columnist fulminated against the new policy for three consecutive weeks.”

Etymology

From Latin fulminare (to lighten, strike with lightning), from fulmen (lightning).
