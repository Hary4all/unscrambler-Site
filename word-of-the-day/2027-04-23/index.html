Sartorial

adjective

Definition

Relating to tailoring, clothes, or style of dress. Sartorial elegance is elegance in the way one dresses.

Example Sentence

“His sartorial choices set him apart from every other executive in the building.”

Etymology

From Latin sartor (tailor), from sarcire (to patch).
