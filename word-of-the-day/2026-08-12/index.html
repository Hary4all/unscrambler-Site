Sagacity

noun

Definition

The ability to make good judgments and decisions; wisdom. A leader's sagacity is proven by the long-term consequences of their choices.

Example Sentence

“The sagacity of the village elder had settled disputes peacefully for over forty years.”

Etymology

From Latin sagacitas, from sagax (shrewd, keen-scented).
