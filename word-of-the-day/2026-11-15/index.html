Lustrousness

noun

Definition

The state of having a gentle sheen or soft glow; radiance. Lustrousness in a surface or person attracts and holds the eye.

Example Sentence

“The lustrousness of the silk gown caught every light in the hall.”

Etymology

From lustre + -ousness, from Old French lustre, from Italian lustro, from lustrare (to illuminate).
