Lacunae

noun

Definition

Gaps or missing parts, especially in a manuscript or text; spaces left by something absent. The plural of lacuna.

Example Sentence

“The historian filled in the lacunae in the medieval record with careful reasoning from adjacent sources.”

Etymology

From Latin lacuna (pool, pit, hole), from lacus (lake).
