Solicitous

adjective

Definition

Characterised by or showing interest or concern; eager and attentive. A solicitous host anticipates every need before it is expressed.

Example Sentence

“The nurses were solicitous and kind, checking on each patient every hour through the long night.”

Etymology

From Latin sollicitus (anxious), from sollus (whole) + citus, past participle of ciere (to set in motion).
