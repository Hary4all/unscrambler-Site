Limpid

adjective

Definition

Perfectly clear; unclouded. Limpid water and limpid prose share a quality of honest transparency.

Example Sentence

“The limpid prose of the essay made it readable long after its subject had ceased to be relevant.”

Etymology

From Latin limpidus, perhaps related to lympha (water).
