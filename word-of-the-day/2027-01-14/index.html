Corpulent

adjective

Definition

Fat; having a large, heavy body. A corpulent person carries significant excess weight.

Example Sentence

“The corpulent merchant wheezed up the steep staircase and collapsed gratefully into the chair.”

Etymology

From Latin corpulentus, from corpus (body).
