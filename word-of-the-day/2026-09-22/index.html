Obstreperous

adjective

Definition

Noisy and difficult to control. An obstreperous crowd makes its displeasure loudly and physically known.

Example Sentence

“The obstreperous toddler refused to sit quietly through the entire ceremony.”

Etymology

From Latin obstreperus, from obstrepere (to make a noise against), from ob- (against) + strepere (to make noise).
