Panoply

noun

Definition

A complete or impressive collection; a splendid display or array. The word originally referred to a full suit of armour.

Example Sentence

“The museum's panoply of ancient weapons stretched the length of the great hall.”

Etymology

From Greek panoplia (complete armour), from pan- (all) + hopla (arms, armour).
