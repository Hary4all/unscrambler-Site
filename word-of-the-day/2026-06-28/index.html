Vociferous

adjective

Definition

Expressing or characterized by vehement opinions; loud and forceful. A vociferous crowd makes its feelings unmistakably known.

Example Sentence

“Vociferous protests outside the parliament building lasted through the night.”

Etymology

From Latin vociferari (to shout), from vox (voice) + ferre (to carry).
