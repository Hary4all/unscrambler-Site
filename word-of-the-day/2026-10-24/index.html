Temerity

noun

Definition

Excessive confidence or boldness; audacity. Temerity implies recklessness — acting without adequate caution.

Example Sentence

“She had the temerity to correct the professor in front of the entire lecture hall.”

Etymology

From Latin temeritas (rashness), from temere (blindly, rashly).
