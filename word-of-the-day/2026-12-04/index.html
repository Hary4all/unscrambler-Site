Ineffable

adjective

Definition

Too great or sacred to express in words; unutterable. Some truths can only be pointed at, not stated.

Example Sentence

“There is an ineffable quality to great music that prevents any description from capturing it.”

Etymology

From Latin ineffabilis, from in- (not) + effabilis (utterable).
