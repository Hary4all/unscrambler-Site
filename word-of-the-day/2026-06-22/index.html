Limpid

adjective

Definition

Completely clear and transparent; free from anything that disturbs clarity. It can describe water, eyes, prose, or anything marked by luminous clarity.

Example Sentence

“The limpid mountain stream revealed every pebble on its bed.”

Etymology

From Latin limpidus, probably related to lympha (water).
