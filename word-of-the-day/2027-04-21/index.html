Aplomb

noun

Definition

Self-confidence or assurance, especially when in a demanding situation. Aplomb is poise under pressure.

Example Sentence

“She handled the hostile questions with aplomb, never losing her composure for a moment.”

Etymology

From French aplomb (perpendicularity), from a plomb (according to the plumb line).
