Vivid

adjective

Definition

Producing powerful feelings or strong, clear images in the mind; strikingly bright or intense. A vivid description plants itself in the memory and refuses to leave.

Example Sentence

“The vivid red of the poppies against the grey sky was something none of them forgot.”

Etymology

From Latin vividus (lively, spirited), from vivere (to live).
