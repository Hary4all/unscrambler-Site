Coruscate

verb

Definition

To flash or sparkle; to show brilliance in exposition or wit. A coruscating speech dazzles the audience with its energy.

Example Sentence

“Her intelligence coruscated throughout the lecture, lighting up every topic she touched.”

Etymology

From Latin coruscare (to vibrate, flash), from coruscus (vibrating).
