Circumlocution

noun

Definition

The use of many words where fewer would do; a roundabout way of saying something. Circumlocution is the enemy of clear, direct communication.

Example Sentence

“His circumlocutions only increased the committee's impatience; they wanted a simple yes or no.”

Etymology

From Latin circumlocutio, from circum- (around) + locutio (speech), from loqui (to speak).
