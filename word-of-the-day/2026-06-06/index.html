Verdant

adjective

Definition

Green with grass or other rich vegetation; inexperienced or unsophisticated. A verdant landscape is one lush with healthy plant life.

Example Sentence

“The verdant hills rolled away beneath a cloudless sky.”

Etymology

From French verdant, from verde (green), from Latin viridis (green).

Scrabble Score

Synonyms & Related Words
