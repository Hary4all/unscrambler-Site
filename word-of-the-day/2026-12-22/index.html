Viridian

noun

Definition

A blue-green pigment or colour; a chromium oxide used in paints. It is a distinctive, rich cool green favoured by painters.

Example Sentence

“She squeezed a long curl of viridian onto the palette and began mixing the sea.”

Etymology

From Latin viridis (green) + -ian.
