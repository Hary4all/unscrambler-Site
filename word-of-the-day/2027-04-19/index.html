Ineffable

adjective

Definition

Beyond description; surpassing words. The best moments of a life are, perhaps, all ineffable.

Example Sentence

“She looked at the completed painting and felt something ineffable — satisfaction, yes, but also something larger and quieter.”

Etymology

From Latin ineffabilis, from in- (not) + effabilis (utterable).
