Fecundity

noun

Definition

The ability to produce many offspring or new ideas; fertility. Intellectual fecundity is the quality that generates one good idea after another.

Example Sentence

“The fecundity of the composer's imagination never seemed to exhaust itself — he produced masterworks until his death.”

Etymology

From Latin fecunditas, from fecundus (fruitful).
