Perseverate

verb

Definition

To repeat or prolong an action, thought, or utterance after the stimulus that prompted it has ceased. In psychology it refers to a symptom of certain conditions.

Example Sentence

“The child continued to perseverate on the subject long after everyone else had moved on.”

Etymology

From Latin perseverare (to persist), from per- (thoroughly) + severus (strict, serious).
