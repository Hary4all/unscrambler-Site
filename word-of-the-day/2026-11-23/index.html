Simulacrum

noun

Definition

An image or representation of someone or something; an unsatisfactory imitation or substitute. It carries a slight suggestion of fakeness or unreality.

Example Sentence

“The resort offered a simulacrum of tropical paradise that most visitors found perfectly adequate.”

Etymology

From Latin simulacrum (likeness, image), from simulare (to simulate), from similis (like).
