Equanimity

noun

Definition

Mental calmness and composure, especially in a difficult situation; evenness of temper. It is a quality admired in Stoic philosophy.

Example Sentence

“She faced the devastating news with remarkable equanimity, consoling those around her.”

Etymology

From Latin aequanimitas, from aequus (equal) + animus (mind).
