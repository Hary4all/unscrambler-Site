Prestidigitation

noun

Definition

The performance of seemingly magical feats by manual dexterity; sleight of hand. The word itself sounds like an elaborate trick.

Example Sentence

“With breathtaking prestidigitation, the magician made the coin vanish from the man's closed fist.”

Etymology

From French prestidigitation, from preste (nimble) + Latin digitus (finger).
