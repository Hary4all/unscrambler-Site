Vivacious

adjective

Definition

Attractively lively and animated; full of life. A vivacious personality makes every gathering brighter.

Example Sentence

“The vivacious young teacher made history feel as alive and immediate as the morning news.”

Etymology

From Latin vivax (long-lived, vigorous), from vivere (to live).
