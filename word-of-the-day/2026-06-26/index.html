Solace

noun

Definition

Comfort or consolation in a time of distress or sadness. It can also be used as a verb meaning to give comfort.

Example Sentence

“She found solace in long walks along the cliffs after the loss of her father.”

Etymology

From Old French solas, from Latin solatium, from solari (to comfort).

Synonyms & Related Words
