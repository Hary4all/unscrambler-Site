Evocative

adjective

Definition

Bringing strong images, memories, or feelings to mind; richly suggestive. Evocative writing transports the reader to another time and place.

Example Sentence

“The smell of baking bread was powerfully evocative of her grandmother's farmhouse kitchen.”

Etymology

From Latin evocare (to call out), from ex- (out) + vocare (to call).
