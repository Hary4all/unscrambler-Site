Vivify

verb

Definition

To enliven or animate; to make more lively, intense, or striking. Good teaching vivifies the material it presents.

Example Sentence

“She had a gift for vivifying the driest historical facts with stories and anecdotes.”

Etymology

From Latin vivificare, from vivus (alive) + facere (to make).
