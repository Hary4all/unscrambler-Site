Acrimony

noun

Definition

Bitterness or ill feeling; sharpness or severity in thought, manner, or speech. When negotiations are described as acrimonious, no one is pleased with the outcome.

Example Sentence

“The divorce proceedings were marked by a level of acrimony that surprised even the lawyers.”

Etymology

From Latin acrimonia (sharpness, pungency), from acer (sharp, keen).
