Incorrigible

adjective

Definition

Not able to be corrected, improved, or reformed. An incorrigible behaviour pattern is one resistant to all attempts at change.

Example Sentence

“He was an incorrigible optimist, cheerful even in the bleakest circumstances.”

Etymology

From Late Latin incorrigibilis, from in- (not) + corrigibilis (correctable), from corrigere (to correct).
