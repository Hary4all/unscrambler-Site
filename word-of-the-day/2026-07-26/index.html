Vicarious

adjective

Definition

Experienced in the imagination through the feelings or actions of another person. Vicarious pleasure is enjoyment had through someone else's experience.

Example Sentence

“She found vicarious excitement in the adventures she read about in her favourite novels.”

Etymology

From Latin vicarius (substitute), from vicis (change, alternation).
