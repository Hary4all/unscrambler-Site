Rapturous

adjective

Definition

Feeling or expressing great pleasure or enthusiasm; ecstatic. A rapturous welcome is one of unbounded joy and excitement.

Example Sentence

“The audience erupted in rapturous applause the moment the curtain fell.”

Etymology

From Latin raptus (seized, carried away), from rapere (to seize).
