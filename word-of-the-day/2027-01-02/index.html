Crepuscular

adjective

Definition

Relating to twilight. The crepuscular hour, that brief interval between day and night, has inspired more poetry than any other time.

Example Sentence

“The crepuscular hours around sunrise are the most productive of the day for many writers.”

Etymology

From Latin crepusculum (twilight), from creper (dusky).
