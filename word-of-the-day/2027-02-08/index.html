Lambent

adjective

Definition

Flickering softly; playing lightly over a surface. Lambent wit illuminates without scorching.

Example Sentence

“The lambent quality of his humour made it possible to discuss the most painful subjects.”

Etymology

From Latin lambens, present participle of lambere (to lick).
