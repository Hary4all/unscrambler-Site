Insouciance

noun

Definition

Casual lack of concern; an easy unconcern. Insouciance, like all freedom, is easier to maintain when nothing is truly at stake.

Example Sentence

“Her insouciance was so complete that it seemed impossible to rattle her, whatever the circumstances.”

Etymology

From French insouciance, from in- (not) + soucier (to care).
