Tenacious

adjective

Definition

Holding firmly to something; persistent and determined. A tenacious person does not give up easily in the face of obstacles.

Example Sentence

“Her tenacious refusal to accept defeat eventually won the court case after eight years.”

Etymology

From Latin tenax (holding fast), from tenere (to hold).
