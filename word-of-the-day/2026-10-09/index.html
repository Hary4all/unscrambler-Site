Legerdemain

noun

Definition

Skillful use of one's hands when performing conjuring tricks; trickery. It describes both literal sleight of hand and metaphorical deception.

Example Sentence

“The accountant's legerdemain with the figures concealed losses for two full quarters.”

Etymology

From French leger de main (light of hand), from leger (light) + de (of) + main (hand), from Latin manus.
