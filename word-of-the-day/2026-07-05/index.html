Ignominious

adjective

Definition

Deserving or causing public disgrace or shame. An ignominious defeat is one that brings deep humiliation.

Example Sentence

“The team suffered an ignominious defeat in their first match of the season.”

Etymology

From Latin ignominiosus, from ignominia (dishonour), from in- (not) + nomen (name).
