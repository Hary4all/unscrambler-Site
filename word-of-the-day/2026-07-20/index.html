Inimitable

adjective

Definition

So good or unusual as to be impossible to copy; unique. An inimitable style is one so distinctive that imitation only highlights the gap.

Example Sentence

“Her inimitable sense of humour had audiences laughing before she had said a word.”

Etymology

From French inimitable, from Latin inimitabilis, from in- (not) + imitabilis (imitable).
