Tremulous

adjective

Definition

Shaking or quivering slightly; timid; nervous. A tremulous voice reveals anxiety or deep emotion.

Example Sentence

“She spoke in a tremulous voice that betrayed the depth of her feeling.”

Etymology

From Latin tremulus, from tremere (to tremble).
