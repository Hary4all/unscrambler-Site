Fecund

adjective

Definition

Producing or capable of producing an abundance of offspring or new growth; fertile. Used figuratively of a mind or imagination that generates many ideas.

Example Sentence

“The fecund imagination of the author produced three novels a year for a decade.”

Etymology

From Latin fecundus (fruitful), from the root of fetus (offspring).
