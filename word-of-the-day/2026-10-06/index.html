Filigree

noun

Definition

Ornamental work of fine twisted threads of gold or silver; intricate, delicate, or complex work. It is used both literally for jewellery and figuratively for any intricate design.

Example Sentence

“The bracelet was decorated with exquisite gold filigree representing flowering vines.”

Etymology

From French filigrane, from Italian filigrana, from Latin filum (thread) + granum (grain).
