Lugubrious

adjective

Definition

Mournful, gloomy, especially to an exaggerated or absurd degree. A lugubrious sigh can be both genuinely sad and slightly comic.

Example Sentence

“He gave a lugubrious sigh and began itemising everything that had gone wrong that week.”

Etymology

From Latin lugubris, from lugere (to mourn).
