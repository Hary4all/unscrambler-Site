Specious

adjective

Definition

Superficially plausible, but actually wrong or misleading. A specious argument looks convincing but will not survive close examination.

Example Sentence

“The proposal rested on a specious analogy that sounded reasonable until anyone thought it through.”

Etymology

From Latin speciosus (fair, beautiful), from species (appearance), from specere (to look).
