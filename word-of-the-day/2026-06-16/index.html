Opalescent

adjective

Definition

Showing many small points of shifting colour against a pale or dark ground; iridescent. The effect resembles the shimmering colours of an opal.

Example Sentence

“The opalescent sky at dusk made it impossible to name a single colour.”

Etymology

From French opalescent, from opale (opal), from Latin opalus, from Greek opallios.
