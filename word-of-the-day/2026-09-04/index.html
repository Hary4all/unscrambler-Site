Insidious

adjective

Definition

Proceeding in a gradual, subtle way but with harmful effects. An insidious disease is one that advances quietly until it is too late to stop.

Example Sentence

“The insidious spread of misinformation can undermine entire communities over time.”

Etymology

From Latin insidiosus (cunning, deceitful), from insidiae (ambush), from insidere (to lie in wait).
