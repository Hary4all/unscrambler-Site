Melisma

noun

Definition

A group of notes sung on one syllable of text; an ornamental vocal flourish. It is a defining feature of gospel, jazz, and traditional Arabic and South Asian singing.

Example Sentence

“Her astonishing melisma on the final chorus brought the entire audience to their feet.”

Etymology

From Greek melisma (song), from melos (song, melody).
