Recrudescence

noun

Definition

The breaking out again of something that has been quiet or latent; a renewed outbreak. A recrudescence of an old problem signals that it was never truly solved.

Example Sentence

“The recrudescence of the disease after two years of remission disappointed the whole medical team.”

Etymology

From Latin recrudescere (to become raw again), from re- (again) + crudescere (to become raw), from crudus (raw).
