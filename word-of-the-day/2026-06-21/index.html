Insouciant

adjective

Definition

Showing a casual lack of concern; carefree and unconcerned. An insouciant attitude suggests an easy confidence that refuses to be troubled.

Example Sentence

“He answered every difficult question with an insouciant smile that infuriated the interviewer.”

Etymology

From French insouciant, from in- (not) + souciant (caring), from se soucier (to care).
