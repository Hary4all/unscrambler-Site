Garrulous

adjective

Definition

Excessively talkative, especially on trivial matters. A garrulous neighbour turns every greeting into a half-hour conversation.

Example Sentence

“The garrulous shopkeeper told every customer the full story of how he had come to open the shop.”

Etymology

From Latin garrulus, from garrire (to chatter).
