Pulchritudinous

adjective

Definition

Having great physical beauty; physically attractive. It is an unusually long word for a simple, very common concept.

Example Sentence

“The contest judges sought the most pulchritudinous entry among dozens of breathtaking photographs.”

Etymology

From Latin pulchritudo (beauty), from pulcher (beautiful).
