Plenitude

noun

Definition

An abundance; a large or sufficient amount. It suggests a fullness and completeness rather than mere sufficiency.

Example Sentence

“The harvest festival celebrated the plenitude of the season's crops.”

Etymology

From Latin plenitudo, from plenus (full).
