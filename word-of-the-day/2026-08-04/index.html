Sesquipedalian

adjective

Definition

Given to using long words; pertaining to a long word. The word itself is an example of what it describes.

Example Sentence

“His sesquipedalian speeches exhausted the patience of the press gallery.”

Etymology

From Latin sesquipedalis (a foot and a half long), from sesqui- (one and a half) + pes (foot).
