Enervate

verb

Definition

To make someone feel drained of energy or vitality; to weaken. Extreme heat enervates both body and mind.

Example Sentence

“The relentless schedule enervated the team until even the most optimistic members began to flag.”

Etymology

From Latin enervare (to weaken), from ex- (out of) + nervus (sinew, nerve).
