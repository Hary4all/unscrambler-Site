Abstruse

adjective

Definition

Difficult to understand; obscure. Abstruse academic writing often confuses more than it illuminates.

Example Sentence

“Even specialists found the philosopher's later work almost comically abstruse.”

Etymology

From Latin abstrusus, past participle of abstrudere (to push away, hide), from ab- (away) + trudere (to push).
