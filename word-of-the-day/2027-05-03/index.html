Recondite

adjective

Definition

Not known by many people; abstruse. Recondite interests are those pursued in the company of very few.

Example Sentence

“His recondite knowledge of Byzantine coinage made him the only person anyone called when such a coin appeared at auction.”

Etymology

From Latin reconditus, past participle of recondere (to hide away).
