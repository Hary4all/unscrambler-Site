Temerity

noun

Definition

Boldness; audacity; reckless confidence. Temerity oversteps where bravery merely goes to the edge.

Example Sentence

“He had the temerity to send his first novel directly to the most prestigious publisher in the country.”

Etymology

From Latin temeritas (rashness), from temere (blindly).
