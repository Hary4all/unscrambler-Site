Verisimilitude

noun

Definition

The appearance of being true or real; the quality that makes fiction feel convincing. Verisimilitude is what makes readers forget they are reading.

Example Sentence

“The period drama achieved its verisimilitude through obsessive attention to costume and language.”

Etymology

From Latin verisimilitudo, from verus (true) + similis (like) + tudo (abstract noun suffix).
