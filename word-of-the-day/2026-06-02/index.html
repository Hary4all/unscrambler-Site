Lambent

adjective

Definition

Glowing, gleaming, or flickering with a soft light; brilliantly playful in expression. It suggests a gentle, luminous quality rather than harsh brightness.

Example Sentence

“The lambent glow of fireflies transformed the garden into something magical.”

Etymology

From Latin lambens, present participle of lambere (to lick), suggesting flames licking gently.
