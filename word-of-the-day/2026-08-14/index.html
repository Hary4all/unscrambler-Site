Dulcet

adjective

Definition

Sweet and soothing, especially in sound. Dulcet tones are gentle and pleasing, never harsh or strident.

Example Sentence

“The dulcet tones of the lute drifted out from the open window.”

Etymology

From Old French doucet, diminutive of doux (sweet), from Latin dulcis (sweet).
