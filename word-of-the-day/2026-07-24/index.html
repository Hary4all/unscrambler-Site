Luminary

noun

Definition

A person who inspires or influences others; an eminent person. Originally it meant a natural light-giving body, such as the sun or moon.

Example Sentence

“The conference brought together luminaries from every field of science and technology.”

Etymology

From Latin luminare (lamp, light), from lumen (light).
