Lassitude

noun

Definition

Tiredness; lack of energy; weariness. Lassitude is more than fatigue — it is a state where the will to move has temporarily abandoned the body.

Example Sentence

“A profound lassitude descended on the survivors of the disaster, who slept for days.”

Etymology

From Latin lassitudo, from lassus (faint, weary).
