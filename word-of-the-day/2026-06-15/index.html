Sycophant

noun

Definition

A person who acts obsequiously toward someone important in order to gain advantage; a flatterer. Sycophancy is universally recognized as a form of dishonesty.

Example Sentence

“Every ruler needs advisors willing to challenge them, not a cabinet full of sycophants.”

Etymology

From Greek sukophantes, possibly from sukon (fig) + phainein (to show), referring to an informer.
