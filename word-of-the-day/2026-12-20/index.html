Serendipity

noun

Definition

The happy occurrence of unplanned discoveries. Life's best turns are often the result of serendipity rather than planning.

Example Sentence

“Sheer serendipity brought the two scientists to the same conference where their ideas clicked.”

Etymology

Coined by Horace Walpole in 1754 after the Persian fairy tale of the Three Princes of Serendip.
