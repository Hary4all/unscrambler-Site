Laborious

adjective

Definition

Requiring considerable time and effort; not particularly skillful or artful. Laborious tasks are demanding and tedious.

Example Sentence

“The laborious process of hand-copying manuscripts was eventually replaced by the printing press.”

Etymology

From Latin laboriosus, from labor (labour, toil).
