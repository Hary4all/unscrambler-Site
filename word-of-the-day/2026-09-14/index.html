Tranquility

noun

Definition

The quality or state of being tranquil; calm; peacefulness. Tranquility is the deep stillness that comes when all disturbance has subsided.

Example Sentence

“The tranquility of the early morning lake was broken only by the call of a heron.”

Etymology

From Latin tranquillitas, from tranquillus (calm, still).
