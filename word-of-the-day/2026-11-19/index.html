Anodyne

adjective

Definition

Not likely to provoke dissent or offence; soothing; painless. An anodyne statement is deliberately bland to avoid controversy.

Example Sentence

“The chairman's anodyne statement of condolences satisfied no one who had actually been affected.”

Etymology

From Latin anodynus, from Greek anodunos (free from pain), from an- (without) + odune (pain).
