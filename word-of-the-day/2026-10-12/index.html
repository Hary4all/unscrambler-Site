Susurrant

adjective

Definition

Whispering; making a soft rustling or murmuring sound. Susurrant describes things that produce a gentle, flowing sound.

Example Sentence

“The susurrant leaves of the silver birch were endlessly calming to walk beneath.”

Etymology

From Latin susurrans, present participle of susurrare (to whisper, hum).
