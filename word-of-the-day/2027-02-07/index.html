Persiflage

noun

Definition

Light and slightly contemptuous mockery or banter; witty raillery. Persiflage is teasing that is sophisticated enough to be amusing rather than wounding.

Example Sentence

“The dinner-table persiflage of the old friends was so rapid that newcomers could barely follow it.”

Etymology

From French persiflage, from persifler (to banter), from per- + siffler (to whistle, hiss).
