Mercurial

adjective

Definition

Subject to sudden or unpredictable changes of mood or mind; lively and clever. The word derives from the swift-moving planet Mercury.

Example Sentence

“Her mercurial temperament kept everyone around her alert — joyful one hour, furious the next.”

Etymology

From Latin mercurialis, of or pertaining to Mercury, messenger of the gods and patron of trade.
