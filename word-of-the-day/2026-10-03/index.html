Taciturn

adjective

Definition

Reserved or uncommunicative in speech; saying little. A taciturn person speaks when spoken to and not always then.

Example Sentence

“The taciturn ranger revealed more about the forest in a single sentence than most guides said in a day.”

Etymology

From Latin taciturnus, from tacitus (silent), from tacere (to be silent).
