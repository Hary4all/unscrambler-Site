Flibbertigibbet

noun

Definition

A frivolous, flighty, or excessively talkative person. The word itself sounds like the person it describes.

Example Sentence

“She was a charming flibbertigibbet whose opinions changed every time she entered a new room.”

Etymology

From Middle English flipergebet, of imitative origin, suggesting rapid, silly chatter.
