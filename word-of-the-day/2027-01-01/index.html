Susurrus

noun

Definition

A whispering or rustling sound; a soft murmur. The susurrus is as close as a word can come to the sound it names.

Example Sentence

“The susurrus of the crowd before the concert began was itself a kind of music.”

Etymology

From Latin susurrus (murmur, whisper).
