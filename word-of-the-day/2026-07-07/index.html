Maelstrom

noun

Definition

A powerful whirlpool; a situation or state of confused movement or violent turmoil. The word comes from Norwegian and refers to a famous tidal current.

Example Sentence

“The young trader was swept into a maelstrom of bad decisions and mounting debt.”

Etymology

From Dutch maelstroom, from malen (to whirl) + stroom (stream).
