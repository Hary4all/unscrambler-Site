Celerity

noun

Definition

Swiftness of movement; speed. Celerity implies not just speed but an efficient, purposeful quickness.

Example Sentence

“The celerity with which the emergency services responded saved many lives.”

Etymology

From Latin celeritas, from celer (swift).
