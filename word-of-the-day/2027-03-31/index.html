Fecund

adjective

Definition

Producing or capable of producing many ideas, books, offspring, or inventions. Fecund minds are the engines of culture.

Example Sentence

“The fecund period following the war produced an extraordinary generation of novelists and painters.”

Etymology

From Latin fecundus (fruitful).
