Ephemeral

adjective

Definition

Very short-lived; transient. Human attention itself has become increasingly ephemeral in the digital age.

Example Sentence

“The ephemeral trends of social media bear little resemblance to lasting cultural change.”

Etymology

From Greek ephemeros, from epi- (on) + hemera (day).
