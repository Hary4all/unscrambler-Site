Mendacity

noun

Definition

Untruthfulness; habitual dishonesty. Mendacity, once established, is almost impossible to rehabilitate from.

Example Sentence

“The investigation into the politician's mendacity ran to several hundred pages.”

Etymology

From Latin mendacitas, from mendax (lying).
