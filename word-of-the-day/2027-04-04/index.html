Melancholy

noun

Definition

A quality of pensive sadness; a gentle, reflective grief. Melancholy at its best is not destructive but clarifying.

Example Sentence

“The music held a melancholy that was not painful to feel — more like a beautiful weight on the chest.”

Etymology

From Greek melankholia, from melas (black) + khole (bile).
