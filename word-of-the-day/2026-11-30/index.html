Fugacious

adjective

Definition

Tending to disappear; fleeting; transitory. Fugacious things pass quickly and leave only a faint trace behind.

Example Sentence

“The fugacious scent of the night-blooming flowers was gone by daybreak.”

Etymology

From Latin fugax (fleeing), from fugere (to flee).
