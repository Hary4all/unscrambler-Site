FAQ

Spelling game questions

Are these spelling games free?

Yes. WordFindLab spelling adventures are free to enjoy.

Can kids play safely?

Yes. The page is built to stay bright, simple, and family-friendly.

Can I use hints?

Yes. Hints help keep the game encouraging and supportive.

Can I share my result?

Yes. You can share on Facebook or copy the page link.

What age is this spelling game good for?

It works well for younger children who are learning letters and simple words, and it also gives older kids a quick spelling warm-up.

Can children replay with different categories?

Yes. Kids can switch between Animals, Food, School, Nature, Family, Colors, Space, and All Categories.

Does the game work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The spelling adventure is designed for mobile, tablet, mouse, and keyboard use.
