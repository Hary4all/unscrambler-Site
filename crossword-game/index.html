How to Play

How to solve the crossword

  • Tap or click a square to select a word.
  • Type letters on desktop or use the mobile keyboard below the grid.
  • Use the clue list to jump directly to a word.
  • Ask for a hint if you get stuck, then check your answer when you want a review.
Why Crossword Puzzles Help

Benefits of playing crosswords

  • Practice spelling, memory, and pattern recognition.
  • Build vocabulary with a quick, replayable challenge.
  • Stay engaged with clues that fit both kids and grown-up players.
  • Use the same puzzle on mouse, keyboard, tablet, or touch screen.

FAQ

Crossword Game FAQ

Is this crossword game free?

Yes. WordFindLab Crossword Game is free to play in your browser.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The game supports touch input and includes a mobile on-screen keyboard.

Can I use keyboard and mouse?

Yes. You can click or tap cells and type letters with a keyboard on desktop.

Are the puzzles different every time?

Yes. New puzzles are generated from the clue bank, and the last ten puzzle signatures are saved so repeats are less likely.

Can kids play this crossword?

Yes. The mini and kids crossword modes are designed to stay bright, friendly, and easy to read.
