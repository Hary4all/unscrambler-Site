Your Spelling Bee

How Spelling Bee works

Each puzzle gives you seven letters arranged in a honeycomb. You make words of four or more letters, every word must use the center letter, and you can use any letter as many times as you like. At least one word, the pangram, uses all seven letters.

How the points add up

  • Four-letter words score 1 point.
  • Longer words score 1 point per letter (a seven-letter word is worth 7).
  • Each pangram gets a 7-point bonus on top of its length.

Tips to reach Genius without a solver

  • Hunt the pangram first. It is worth the most and often unlocks related words. Try common endings such as -ING, -TION, -ED and -ER if the letters allow.
  • Work letter by letter. Use our hint grid: if it says there are five words starting with T, list T-words until you find them all.
  • Add endings. If you found PLANT, check PLANTER, and if there is an I, N and G, try PLANTING.
  • Double up. Letters can repeat, so look for words like LEVEL, TATTER or ALPACA.
  • Take a break. Coming back later is surprisingly effective — your brain keeps working on the letters.

Frequently asked questions

What are the rules of Spelling Bee?

Make words of four or more letters using the seven letters in the hive. Every word must include the center letter, and letters can be used more than once. A pangram is a word that uses all seven letters at least once.

How is Spelling Bee scored?

Four-letter words are worth 1 point. Longer words score 1 point per letter, so a six-letter word is worth 6. A pangram earns an extra 7 points on top of its length.

Why does the solver show more words than the puzzle accepts?

The full dictionary includes rare, technical and archaic words. The daily puzzle uses a smaller list of common words, so start with the Common words group, which matches the puzzle much more closely.

How do I use the hints without spoilers?

Leave Hint mode on. You will see how many words start with each letter and how long they are, plus the number of pangrams, without seeing any answers. Click Reveal words when you are ready.

Is WordFindLab connected to The New York Times?

No. WordFindLab is an independent, free word tool. Spelling Bee is a puzzle published by The New York Times; this page simply helps you find words that fit its rules.
