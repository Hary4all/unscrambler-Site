How the Countdown letters round works

One contestant chooses nine letters, asking for a vowel or a consonant each time, and the selection must include at least three vowels and at least four consonants. When the clock starts, both players have 30 seconds to make the longest word they can. Each letter tile can be used only once, so with one E on the board you can’t play a word with two Es.

Scoring

  • A valid word scores one point per letter: a seven-letter word is worth 7.
  • A nine-letter word scores double, 18 points.
  • Only the longer valid word scores. If both players offer words of the same length, both score.
  • The final conundrum is a nine-letter anagram worth 10 points to whoever buzzes in first with the right answer.

Tips for longer words

  • Look for endings first. -ING, -ED, -ER, -EST, -ION and -LY turn a short word into a long one. With I, N and G on the board, try every root word that fits in front.
  • Pick letters wisely. Three or four vowels and common consonants such as R, S, T, L and N give you the most options. Too many awkward letters (J, Q, X, Z, V) make long words rare.
  • Learn common prefixes. RE-, UN-, DE- and IN- add length to words you already know.
  • Write as you think. Jot down a safe six-letter word early, then hunt for something longer.
  • Practise daily. Deal a random round above every day — speed comes from seeing lots of letter combinations.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Countdown letters round work?

A contestant picks nine letters, choosing vowel or consonant for each, with at least three vowels and four consonants. Both players then have 30 seconds to make the longest word they can from those letters, using each tile only once.

How is the letters round scored?

A valid word scores one point per letter, and a nine-letter word scores double: 18 points. Only the player with the longer valid word scores; if both words are the same length, both players score.

What is the Countdown conundrum?

The conundrum is the final round: nine scrambled letters that form a single nine-letter word. The first player to buzz in with the right answer scores 10 points.

Why does the solver show words Countdown might not allow?

Our dictionary is very large and includes rare and technical words. Countdown checks words against its own dictionary, so start with the Common words group, which is much closer to what is accepted on the show.

Is WordFindLab connected to Countdown or Channel 4?

No. WordFindLab is an independent, free word tool. Countdown is a Channel 4 programme; this page simply helps you find words that fit the letters round.
