5-Letter Words in Wordle

Wordle uses 5-letter words exclusively. Every answer is a common English word exactly 5 letters long. The game's dictionary contains around 2,300 answer words, all of which are in the list above. When you know certain letters are in the answer (yellow clues) or in specific positions (green clues), use the filter bar above — type the known letters into "Starts with", "Ends with", or "Contains" to narrow your options down to just a handful.

5-Letter Words in Scrabble

In Scrabble, 5-letter words are versatile plays — long enough to score well but short enough to fit most board positions. They don't qualify for the 50-point bingo bonus (that requires 7 tiles), but a 5-letter word hitting a Triple Word Score with a high-value tile can still score 40–60 points. The best 5-letter Scrabble words combine J, Q, X, or Z with common vowels.

Common Questions

How many 5-letter words are there in English?
There are 15,921 five-letter words in the standard English dictionary (words_alpha). The Wordle answer list uses a subset of around 2,300 common words.
What is the best 5-letter word for Wordle?
Top picks are CRANE, SLATE, RAISE, STARE, AUDIO, AROSE — they cover the most common letters (A, E, R, S, T) across multiple positions. There's no single "best" word, but any opener with 5 unique letters and 2+ vowels is strong.
