How WordFindLab works + tips

Enter up to 15 letters — including up to 3 blank wildcards (?). The tool generates every valid combination of those letters and matches them against a dictionary of English words. Results are grouped by length, with Scrabble point values shown on each word so you can spot the highest-scoring plays instantly.

  • Add ? for a blank tile and let the solver test every fit.
  • Use Starts with and Ends with to tighten a crossword slot.
  • Switch to Common words for a faster, cleaner play list.

Works for every word game

Whether you need a word finder, letter matcher, or anagram solver, the same engine handles them all — Scrabble, Words With Friends, Wordscapes, Boggle, crosswords, and daily word puzzles. Use the advanced filters to narrow results by starting letters, ending letters, or exact word length.
