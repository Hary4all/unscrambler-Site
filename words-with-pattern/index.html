Matching words

How to use the word pattern finder

Letter pattern search: enter _ (or ?) for unknown letters, e.g. _A_E_ finds BAKED, CAGED, and PAVED. Each blank stands for exactly one letter, so the pattern also sets the word length.

Any length: use * to match any number of letters. PRE* finds every word starting with PRE, and *TION finds every word ending in TION.

Narrow it down: add letters to Must include when you know a letter is in the word but not where, and to Exclude when you know a letter is not in the word — exactly how Wordle's yellow and gray tiles work.

Word pattern examples

Crossword clue with 5 squares and the 2nd and 4th letters filled in? Type a word pattern like _R_N_. Know a 7-letter answer ends in ING? Try ____ING. Every word pattern works the same way: known letters stay in place, blanks fill in the rest.

What is a word pattern finder?
A word pattern finder lists every word that fits a letter pattern you enter. Type the letters you know in their positions and use _ or ? for each unknown letter.
How do I search a letter pattern?
Enter your letter pattern with _ or ? for unknown letters. For example, _A_E_ finds 5-letter words like BAKED, CAGED, and PAVED. Use * to match any number of letters, such as PRE* for every word starting with PRE.
Can I exclude letters I know are wrong?
Yes. Type the letters to leave out in the Exclude box, which is useful for Wordle when you already know which letters are gray.
Does this word pattern finder help with crosswords?
Yes. Enter the crossing letters you already have and _ for the empty squares, and the list shows every word that fits the space.
