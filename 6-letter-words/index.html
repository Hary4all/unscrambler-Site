About Six-Letter Words

Six-letter words are medium-length words in English. There are 29,874 of them in the standard dictionary. In Scrabble, 6-letter words can score well on premium squares, and words in this range are common on the board.

How many 6-letter words are in English?
There are 29,874 words that are exactly 6 letters long in the standard English dictionary.
