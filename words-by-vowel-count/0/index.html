Why this page is useful

Zero-vowel words are useful for challenge boards because they eliminate a lot of vowel-heavy noise and leave you with clean, uncommon letter shapes.

It is especially useful when you want to compare vowel balance across board-game plays, clue answers, or search results without having to sift through every unrelated word family.

Frequently asked questions

Do you count Y as a vowel here?
No. This family counts the five standard vowels only: A, E, I, O, and U.
Why are zero-vowel words useful?
They can help with rare-letter racks, challenge rounds, and tricky crossword or Scrabble-style boards.
