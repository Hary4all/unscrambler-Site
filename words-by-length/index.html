Frequently asked questions

Which word length has the most English words?

9-letter words are the biggest group in our dictionary (53,402), with 8- and 10-letter words close behind.

How many 5-letter words are there?

Our dictionary lists 15,921 five-letter words. Wordle uses a much smaller set of common ones as answers.

Why do 2-letter words matter in Scrabble?

They let you play alongside other words and score in two directions at once.
