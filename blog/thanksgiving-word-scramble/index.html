How to solve a word scramble fast

  • Find the easy letter pairs first. Look for common pairs such as TH, CH, SH, ER, ING and LE, and put them together.
  • Try the vowels in the middle. Most short words follow a consonant–vowel pattern, so spread the vowels out.
  • Think about the theme. Every word here is about Thanksgiving, so ask “what Thanksgiving thing has these letters?”
  • Count the letters. A 5-letter scramble needs a 5-letter answer, which rules out a lot of guesses.
  • Still stuck? Type the letters into our free word unscrambler or anagram solver and it will show the answer and every other word the letters make.

Thanksgiving word scramble game ideas

  • Race the clock: give each player a printed copy and see who solves the most in five minutes.
  • Team relay: split into two teams; each player solves one word, then passes the sheet on.
  • Big-word challenge: see how many smaller words everyone can make from THANKSGIVING or CORNUCOPIA, then check with the unscrambler.
  • Make your own: pick words from our thanksgiving words list, mix up the letters, and swap puzzles with a friend.

It is a puzzle where the letters of a Thanksgiving word are mixed up, and you rearrange them to find the word. For example, VAGYR unscrambles to GRAVY.
