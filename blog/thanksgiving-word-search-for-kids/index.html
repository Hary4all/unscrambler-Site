More Thanksgiving words for word searches

Want a different puzzle? Pick 8 to 12 words from the level that suits your child and type them into our printable word search maker.

Easy (ages 4–6): pie, corn, yam, nut, hat, leaf, fall, food, feast, bread.

Medium (ages 6–8): turkey, family, thanks, apple, gravy, maple, acorn, parade, dinner, squash, autumn, pumpkin.

Harder (ages 8–11): harvest, pilgrim, grateful, thankful, cranberry, stuffing, together, November, Thursday, tradition, gratitude, Mayflower.

Challenge (ages 10+): cornucopia, celebration, abundance, appreciation, Wampanoag, Plymouth, generosity, festivities.

Tips for a smoother word search

  • For ages 4–6, use 6–8 short words and only left-to-right and top-to-bottom directions.
  • Read the word list aloud first so younger kids know what they are looking for.
  • Let kids circle words with a different color each time — it turns into a coloring game too.
  • For older kids, add a timer or a race between cousins.
  • Ask kids to use three found words in a sentence about what they are thankful for.

Frequently asked questions

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

In the United States, Thanksgiving 2026 is on Thursday, November 26. Canadian Thanksgiving was on Monday, October 12, 2026.

Yes. Click Print this puzzle and your browser prints only the puzzle and the word list. You can print as many copies as you need for home or class.

What age is this Thanksgiving word search for?

The 12-word, 12 by 12 puzzle suits most kids aged 6 to 10. For younger kids, make an easier version with the easy word list and fewer words.
