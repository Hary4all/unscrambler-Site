Fall Words From A to Z

A list of 100+ fall and autumn words from A to Z, plus describing words, 5-letter fall words for Wordle, and easy ways to use them in class, writing and word games.

Fall is full of great words: crisp, golden, crunchy, cozy. Whether you are a teacher building an autumn word wall, a parent looking for a seasonal spelling list, or a word-game fan hunting for a themed Wordle guess, here are over 100 fall words from A to Z, plus describing words and five-letter options.

Fall words from A to Z

A: acorn, apple, autumn, amber, aroma.

B: bonfire, boots, brown, bushel, blustery.

C: cider, crisp, chilly, cozy, cornstalk, cranberry, chestnut, crunchy.

D: drizzle, deer, dusk, decorate.

E: equinox, evening, earthy.

F: foliage, frost, flannel, football, fireplace, feast.

G: gourd, golden, gather, gratitude, geese.

H: harvest, hayride, hazelnut, Halloween, hoodie.

J: jacket, jack-o'-lantern.

K: knit, kindling.

L: leaves, lantern, layers, latte.

M: maple, mittens, migration, mushroom.

N: November, nutmeg, nuts.

O: October, orange, orchard, owl, oak.

P: pumpkin, pie, pecan, pinecone, plaid, potluck.

Q: quilt, quince.

R: rake, red, rustic, roast, rain.

S: scarecrow, squash, sweater, scarf, September, squirrel, spice.

T: turkey, Thanksgiving, thankful, twig.

W: walnut, wool, windy, wreath, wheat.

Y: yarn, yams, yellow.

Letters like I, U, V, X and Z have very few true fall words, so we have left them out rather than stretch.

Describing words for fall

Great for writing prompts and poems:

  • Weather: crisp, chilly, breezy, blustery, frosty, foggy, misty, damp.
  • Colors: golden, amber, rusty, crimson, copper, burnt orange, russet.
  • Sounds and textures: crunchy, crackling, rustling, cozy, woolly, fuzzy.
  • Smells and tastes: spicy, smoky, sweet, earthy, buttery, cinnamon-scented.

Fall words for kids

For younger children, start with short, picture-friendly words: leaf, rake, owl, pie, nut, red, hat, corn, fall, apple. Older kids can try scarecrow, squirrel, harvest, foliage, migration and equinox. Our free spelling games and online word search make practice feel like play.

Frequently asked questions

What are some fall words?

Popular fall words include autumn, leaves, pumpkin, harvest, acorn, cider, sweater, scarecrow, maple, frost, cozy and crisp.

What are 5-letter fall words?

Acorn, amber, apple, brown, chill, cider, crisp, feast, frost, gourd, gravy, maple, pecan, quilt, roast, scarf, spice, twigs and wheat.

What is the difference between fall and autumn?

They mean the same season. Fall is more common in American English, and autumn is more common in British English.

When does fall start?

In the Northern Hemisphere, astronomical fall begins at the September equinox, around September 22 or 23, and ends at the December solstice. Meteorological fall runs from September 1 to November 30.
