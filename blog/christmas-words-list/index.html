Christmas Words From A to Z

97 Christmas words from A to Z, plus describing words for cards and 5-letter Christmas words for Wordle. Free holiday word lists for kids, teachers and families.

Christmas has some of the most joyful words in English: jolly, merry, twinkling, cozy. Whether you are writing holiday cards, building a December word wall, or hunting for a festive Wordle guess, here are 97 Christmas words from A to Z, plus describing words and five-letter options.

Christmas words from A to Z

A: angel, advent, aglow.

B: bells, bows, baubles, boughs, bake, Bethlehem.

C: carol, candle, candy cane, chimney, cookies, cheer, chestnut, cinnamon.

D: December, decorate, delight.

E: elf, eggnog, evergreen, eve.

F: festive, family, fireplace, frankincense, fruitcake.

G: gift, gingerbread, garland, greetings, glitter, gold.

H: holly, holiday, hope, ham, hymn.

I: icicle, ivy.

J: jolly, joy, jingle.

K: kindness, kings.

L: lights, lantern, list, lullaby.

M: mistletoe, manger, merry, myrrh, mittens.

N: nativity, noel, nutcracker, naughty, nice.

O: ornament.

P: presents, poinsettia, peppermint, pudding, pageant.

Q: quiet, quilt.

R: reindeer, ribbon, rejoice.

S: Santa, sleigh, stocking, star, snow, snowman, sparkle, sugarplum.

T: tinsel, tree, toys, tidings, tradition, turkey.

U: unwrap.

V: vacation, velvet.

W: wreath, wrapping, wish, workshop, wonder.

X: Xmas.

Y: yule, yuletide.

There are no common Christmas words that start with Z, so for word games try words with Z instead.

Describing words for Christmas

Perfect for cards, letters to Santa and holiday writing:

  • Feelings: merry, jolly, joyful, cheerful, grateful, peaceful, hopeful.
  • Sights: twinkling, sparkling, glittering, shimmering, glowing, snowy, red, green, golden.
  • Smells and tastes: spicy, sweet, minty, buttery, warm, cinnamon-scented.
  • Atmosphere: festive, cozy, magical, bright, snug, silent, holy.

5-letter Christmas words for Wordle

Want a festive guess? These five-letter Christmas words are all real words: ANGEL, BELLS, CANDY, CAROL, CHEER, ELVES, FROST, GIFTS, HOLLY, JOLLY, LIGHT, MERRY, MYRRH, STARS, TREAT. Words with common letters such as CAROL, STARS or TREAT make strong first guesses (MYRRH, with no A, E, I, O or U, is a fun one to know). Then use our Wordle solver to narrow it down. Christmas words for kids

For younger children, start with short, picture-friendly words: elf, joy, toy, star, tree, bell, gift, bow, snow, sled. Older kids can try reindeer, ornament, nutcracker, poinsettia, tradition and frankincense. Our free spelling games and online word search are good screen-time picks for the school holidays. Frequently asked questions

What are some Christmas words?

Popular Christmas words include Santa, sleigh, reindeer, tinsel, ornament, wreath, stocking, mistletoe, carol, gingerbread, jolly and merry.

What are 5-letter Christmas words?

Angel, bells, candy, carol, cheer, elves, frost, gifts, holly, jolly, light, merry, myrrh, stars and treat.

What day is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 falls on Friday, December 25, 2026. Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24, and Advent begins on Sunday, November 29, 2026.

Is there a Christmas word that starts with X?

Xmas, a common short form of Christmas, is the best-known one. For word games, try our words with X list.
