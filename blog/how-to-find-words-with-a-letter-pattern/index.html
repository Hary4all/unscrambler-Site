How to Find Words With a Letter Pattern

Use a word pattern search to solve crosswords and Wordle: how blanks and wildcards work, real examples like _A_E_ (540 words), and tips to narrow results fast.

October 9, 2026 | 6 min read | By WordFindLab

WORD PATTERNS

Stuck on a crossword with three letters filled in, or a Wordle with two greens? A word pattern search is the fastest way out. You type the letters you know in their positions, mark the unknown spots, and get every word that fits. Here's how to use one well.

How a letter pattern works

A letter pattern is just the word with blanks. On WordFindLab's word pattern finder, use _ or ? for one unknown letter and * for any number of letters.

Because each blank is exactly one letter, the pattern also fixes the word length. _A_E_ only returns 5-letter words with A second and E fourth.

  • _ or ? = exactly one unknown letter.
  • * = any number of letters (including none).
  • Known letters stay locked in their position.

Real examples (with counts from our dictionary)

Some patterns are wide open, others narrow fast. These counts come straight from the 359,039-word dictionary WordFindLab uses:

  • _A_E_ — 540 words, including GAMES, PAPER, WATER, and TAKEN.
  • _R_N_ — 115 words, including PRINT, FRONT, BRAND, and WRONG.
  • S___E — 229 words, including STATE, SPACE, SCORE, and STAGE.
  • C__T — 34 words, including COST, CART, CHAT, and COAT.
  • __ING — 45 words, including BRING, THING, SWING, and DOING.

Using patterns for crosswords

In a crossword you usually know the length and some crossing letters. Type the squares left to right with blanks for the empty ones. For a 7-letter answer ending in ING, try ____ING — that's 1,779 candidates, so use the clue to pick a theme, then add any crossing letter you're confident about.

Each extra letter you lock in cuts the list dramatically, so fill in the easiest crossing answers first.

  • Solve crossing clues first to get more fixed letters.
  • Use the clue's tense and number: past-tense clues often end in ED, plurals in S.
  • Try * when you're unsure of the length: *TION or PRE*.

Using patterns for Wordle

Wordle gives you three kinds of information, and a good pattern search uses all three. Green letters go into the pattern, yellow letters go into “must include”, and gray letters go into “exclude”.

For example, with green A in position 2 and green E in position 4, start from _A_E_. If S and T came back gray, excluding them removes words like TAKES and SAFER, shrinking the list quickly.

  • Greens → the pattern.
  • Yellows → Must include.
  • Grays → Exclude.

Frequently asked questions

What is a word pattern search?

A word pattern search lists every word that fits a pattern of known letters and blanks, such as _A_E_ for 5-letter words with A second and E fourth.

How many words match _A_E_?

540 words in WordFindLab's 359,039-word dictionary match _A_E_, including GAMES, PAPER, and WATER.

Can I use a letter pattern for Wordle?

Yes. Put green letters in the pattern, yellow letters in Must include, and gray letters in Exclude to narrow the list fast.
