More Christmas words for word searches

Want a new puzzle each day of December? Pick 8 to 12 words from the level that suits your child and type them into our printable word search maker.

Easy (ages 4–6): star, tree, elf, bell, gift, snow, toy, hat, sock, bow.

Medium (ages 6–8): santa, candy, cookie, wreath, sleigh, angel, merry, holly, carol, ribbon, mitten, winter.

Hard (ages 8–11): reindeer, snowman, stocking, present, ornament, chimney, gingerbread, nutcracker, mistletoe, poinsettia.

Christmas word search ideas for home and class

  • Advent puzzle: make one small puzzle a day from December 1 to 24 with three or four new words each time.
  • Class party: print one copy per child and give a sticker to everyone who finds all 12 words.
  • Family race: print two copies and see who finishes first — kids against grown-ups.
  • Spelling practice: after the puzzle, ask kids to write each word from memory. Our spelling games help with the tricky ones.
  • Word challenge: how many words can you make from CHRISTMAS or SNOWFLAKE?

Frequently asked questions

When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 is on Friday, December 25. Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24.

Is this Christmas word search free to print?

Yes. Click Print this puzzle and your browser prints only the puzzle and the word list. Print as many copies as you need for home or class.

What age is this Christmas word search for?

The 12-word, 12 by 12 puzzle suits most kids aged 6 to 10. Words only run left to right, top to bottom, or diagonally down, so there are no backwards words. For younger kids, make an easier puzzle with the easy word list.

Can I make my own Christmas word search?

Yes. Type your own words, such as family names or gift ideas, into the WordFindLab printable word search maker, choose a grid size, and print it. It is free and needs no sign-up.
