What the key Hanukkah words mean

  • Hanukkah — a Hebrew word meaning “dedication”. It remembers the rededication of the Temple in Jerusalem after the Maccabees’ revolt in the 2nd century BCE.
  • Menorah — the candle holder lit each night. The Hanukkah menorah (also called a hanukkiah) holds eight candles plus a helper candle.
  • Shamash — the “helper” candle used to light the others.
  • Dreidel — a four-sided spinning top. Its Hebrew letters nun, gimel, hei and shin stand for “a great miracle happened there”. In Israel, the last letter is pe, for “here”.
  • Latke — a fried potato pancake. Foods fried in oil remember the story of the oil that lasted eight days.
  • Gelt — Yiddish for money; today often chocolate coins used in dreidel games.
  • Sufganiyot — jelly-filled doughnuts, popular in Israel at Hanukkah.
  • Kislev — the Hebrew month in which Hanukkah begins, on the 25th.

Hanukkah 2026 dates

In 2026, Hanukkah begins at sundown on Friday, December 4, when the first candle is lit. The eighth and final candle is lit on the evening of Friday, December 11, and the holiday ends at nightfall on Saturday, December 12, 2026. Because the Hebrew calendar follows the moon, the dates move each year on the regular calendar.

Frequently asked questions

What are some Hanukkah words?

Common Hanukkah words include menorah, dreidel, latke, gelt, shamash, candle, oil, miracle, Maccabees, Jerusalem, temple and dedication.

How do you spell Hanukkah?

Hanukkah and Chanukah are the two most common spellings. Both are English versions of the same Hebrew word, so other forms such as Hanukah are also seen.

When is Hanukkah 2026?

Hanukkah 2026 begins at sundown on Friday, December 4, and ends at nightfall on Saturday, December 12, 2026.

What are 5-letter Hanukkah words?

Brave, Coins, Eight, Flame, Gifts, Gimel, Jelly, Kugel, Latke, Light, Olive, Rabbi, Unity.

What does Hanukkah mean?

Hanukkah means “dedication” in Hebrew. It celebrates the rededication of the Second Temple in Jerusalem.
