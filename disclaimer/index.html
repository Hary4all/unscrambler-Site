Site Disclaimer

How to use WordFindLab responsibly.

WordFindLab is designed to help you explore words, compare scores, and learn better puzzle habits. The site is useful, but it is still informational rather than authoritative. This page explains how we handle results, advertising, and external links so the site stays trustworthy and clear.

Quick summary

  • Results may vary by game edition, dictionary, or board state.
  • Word lists are helpful references, not legal or academic authorities.
  • Ads may appear in the layout, but they do not change the content rules.
  • External links are provided for convenience and may change over time.

What this site does and does not guarantee

Informational use WordFindLab is built to help with solving, learning, and comparing word options. It is not a substitute for official game rules, tournament rulings, or a licensed dictionary publisher.
Scoring and rules Scrabble, Words With Friends, Wordle, and crossword-style games can differ by region or edition. If a final decision matters, confirm it with the official rules or source for your game.
Advertising disclosure The site may display advertising to support the free tools. Ads are placed so the page remains usable, and ad presence does not affect the editorial purpose of the content.
External links When we link to another site, we do so to help users find relevant information. We cannot guarantee the accuracy, availability, or policies of external websites.

Good habits for readers and players

Use the solver pages as a starting point, not the final answer in every situation. When the board, clue, or puzzle format matters, read the surrounding context and use the tool output as a guide. That approach gives you better results and a stronger understanding of the game over time.

If you notice a result that looks unusual, treat it as a prompt to review the word, the spelling, or the puzzle rules. The site is meant to support learning and speed, not replace judgement.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rely on the results for tournament play?

Use the tool as a helper, then check the official dictionary or rule set used by your event.

Do ads change the site’s recommendations?

No. Ads are separate from the editorial content and the solver logic.

What should I do if I find an error?

Send the page URL and a short description through the Contact page so we can review it.
