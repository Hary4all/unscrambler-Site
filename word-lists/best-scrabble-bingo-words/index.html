What is a Scrabble Bingo?

A bingo is when you play all 7 tiles from your rack in a single turn, earning a 50-point bonus on top of the word's base score. Expert players keep "bingo-friendly" racks - sets of tiles that combine easily into 7-letter words. Common bingo stems include SATINE, SATIRE, RETINA, and ARIOSE.

How do I set up bingo plays in Scrabble?
Keep 2-3 common letters (E, R, S, T, N, A) after each turn. Avoid holding duplicates of low-value tiles. Study 7-letter words with common endings like -ING, -TION, -ED.
