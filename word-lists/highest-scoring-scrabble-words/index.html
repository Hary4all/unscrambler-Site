Why High Base Scores Matter

A word's base score is its "floor" - the minimum you'll score even with no board bonuses. High base-score words (those with Q, Z, J, X, and long length) become devastating when played on premium squares. A 40-point base word on a Triple Word Score scores 120 points in a single play.

What is the highest possible Scrabble score?
The theoretical highest single-word score is over 1,700 points (OXYPHENBUTAZONE across three Triple Word Score squares). In practice, 100-150 points for a single play is exceptional.
Do longer words always score more?
Not necessarily - a short word with high-value tiles (like JAZZ or QUIZ) can outscore a long word made of common letters. The combination of word length, tile values, and board position determines the final score.
