Why No-Repeat Letters Matter in Wordle

When a 5-letter word has all unique letters, a single guess tests 5 distinct letters simultaneously. If Wordle responds with all gray tiles, you've eliminated 5 letters at once — the most efficient possible outcome. This is why experienced players almost never open with words like SLEEP or ADDED that repeat a letter.
