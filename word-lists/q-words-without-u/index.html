Why Q-Without-U Words Are Essential

The Q tile scores 10 points - the highest in Scrabble. But Q almost always pairs with U, and if there are no U tiles available on your rack or the board, a Q becomes unplayable "dead weight." Knowing Q-without-U words like QI (12 pts), QOPH (18 pts), and QANAT (17 pts) lets you convert a difficult rack into a high-scoring play.

QI is the most commonly used Q-without-U word in Scrabble - it means the circulating life force in Chinese philosophy and scores 11 base points as a 2-letter word.

What is the most useful Q word without U?
QI is the most useful - it's only 2 letters, scores 11 points, and fits in tight board spaces. QOPH (18 pts) and QANAT (17 pts) are strong longer options.
How many Q words without U are valid in Scrabble?
There are 106 Q-without-U words in the standard English dictionary. Not all are accepted in tournament Scrabble - check the Official Scrabble Players Dictionary (OSPD) for official rulings.
